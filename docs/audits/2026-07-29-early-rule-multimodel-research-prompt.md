# Public-source research task: Taiwan NHI drug reimbursement rules, 1995–1998

You are one read-only research worker. Nested delegation is forbidden. Search
public sources aggressively but lawfully. Do not bypass access controls,
CAPTCHAs, rate limits, or authentication. Do not merely restate search snippets.

## Goal

Find the earliest recoverable source texts and provenance for Taiwan's drug
reimbursement restrictions during ROC years 84–87 (1995–1998), especially the
state effective during ROC year 85 (1996). Determine whether the original
rule text is publicly available as:

1. a born-digital official document;
2. an official or library digitized scan;
3. a catalog record without accessible full text;
4. a later official transcription or retrospective reproduction; or
5. a paper-only holding not yet digitized.

Do not collapse these states.

## Known evidence to challenge or extend

- NHI's later official publication says the instrument was named
  `全民健康保險藥品使用規範` on 1995-06-20 and was reorganized/renamed
  `全民健康保險藥品給付規定` on 1998-03-04.
- A National Central Library / Taiwan Memory catalog record exists for a
  Taiwan Provincial Department of Health forwarding letter:
  document number `84衛技字第052484號`, published 1995-07, forwarding
  `全民健康保險藥品使用規範 民84.6.20`, generally effective 1995-07-01.
  The public catalog identifier is `D9507418`.
- Later official rule texts retain source annotations such as `85/1/1` and
  `85/10/1`; those annotations prove historical states but can miss clauses
  later deleted in full.
- The MOHW FINT exact-phrase query for the later name
  `藥品給付規定` yields no 1996–1998 notices and begins in 1999. This is not
  proof that the earlier instrument or amendments did not exist.

Search name and OCR variants, including:

- 全民健康保險藥品使用規範

- 民84.6.20 / 84年6月20日 / 1995-06-20
- 85/1/1, 85/10/1, 86/1/1 together with specific old categories such as
  albumin, erythropoietin/EPO, anti-microbial drugs, total parenteral
  nutrition/TPN, growth hormone, lipid-lowering agents, liver protectants,
  vitamin D3, calcitonin, and injection-use principles.

Inspect, where searchable:

- National Central Library / Taiwan Memory and other library catalogs;
- Taiwan Provincial Government Gazette or archival holdings;
- Presidential Office Gazette and Executive Yuan Gazette;
- NHI, MOHW, and legacy Bureau of National Health Insurance publications;
- Government Research Bulletin / government publication catalogs;
- university, hospital, medical association, and pharmacy association
  repositories, but clearly label non-official copies;
- Internet Archive or archived legacy government URLs, only when publicly
  accessible.
